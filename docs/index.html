A LITTLE EXPLORATION

Find your way to
the treasure.

Explore five short worlds. In each one, move your character to the treasure chest.

Move one step at a time. Use the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen buttons. North is always up.

Look around as you go. You can see two tiles in each direction. The rest of the world is hidden, including places you have left.

Check your abilities in each world. Your ability card explains whether you can jump fences, swim, or cross lava. These abilities apply to your character in that world.

Use the directions when provided. Try to reach the chest in as few moves as you can. You may need to find your own way. Each world ends at the chest or after 400 attempted moves.

Your moves and timing are saved as you go, including if you leave early. Please use a study ID, not your name.
