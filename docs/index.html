A LITTLE EXPLORATION

Find your way to
the treasure.

Complete one short navigation task. Move your character to the treasure chest. The study takes about 3 minutes.

Move one step at a time. Use the arrow keys, WASD, or the on-screen buttons. North is always up.

Look around as you go. You can see two tiles in each direction. The rest of the world is hidden, including places you have left.

Check your abilities. Your ability card explains whether you can jump fences, swim, or cross lava. These abilities apply to your character in that world.

The speaker may not know your abilities. Some tasks include directions, and others do not. When directions are provided, the speaker may suggest a route that requires abilities your character does not have. Use what you see and your abilities to decide where to go. Try to reach the chest in as few moves as you can. The task ends at the chest or after 400 attempted moves.

Your moves and timing are saved as you go, including if you leave early.
